What we stitch

Every suit made to your body, your occasion, your fabric

Whether it's a daily-wear cotton set or a heavy wedding ensemble, each piece is cut fresh for the person wearing it.

01

Everyday Salwar Suits

Cotton, lawn and linen sets stitched for comfort — straight cut, Patiala, or Anarkali, ready in 4–5 days.

02

Palazzo & Co-ord Sets

Contemporary silhouettes for work and outings, tailored to sit right at the waist and fall correctly at the ankle.

03

Wedding & Festive Wear

Heavy dupatta suits, sharara sets and reception outfits, with hand-finished hems and lining that holds embroidery.

04

Alterations & Refitting

Bring an old suit that no longer fits — we let it out, take it in, or rework the neckline while you wait.

How it works

From fabric to finished suit in four steps

i

Bring your fabric

Or pick from our in-house cottons and silks — we'll tell you honestly how much you need.

ii

Get measured

A proper fitting session, not a guess. Your card is saved, so next time takes minutes.

iii

We cut and stitch

Your suit is cut individually — never from a standard block pattern.

iv

Final fitting

One trial before you take it home, with any small adjustment made on the spot.
